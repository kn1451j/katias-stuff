Telic and atelic
pages: 3876.jpg

I often struggle to reconcile my lived experience with my ideals.

The telic and atelic division of life is interesting, because it poses this
contrast between two spheres, with tension. I would say that an atelic life is
possible. This would be perhaps a religious / zen life. And a fully telic life is
also possible, perhaps this is a "greedy" life.

And I would argue that both are equally as satisfying — perhaps the latter is
often faced w/ regrets but this can also not be unprocessed.

(if u are in the middle, w/ goal in mind, no such state exists).

I wonder if the baselines of satisfaction are different? Does everyone start at 0?

I think we're fooled into thinking one matters by the telic vs atelic oscillation
of life. The more energy spent into telic activities, the more satisfaction we get
from atelic.

What is my utopia? Is it really any better than my life now?
