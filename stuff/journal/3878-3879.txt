A lot vs a little

The reason I struggle with guilt and indecisiveness is because I haven't
established my values. What do I believe is right? What is my moral code? I have
nothing to refer to. I have no religion. But when I love, I take my codes from the
things that I love, and the things I like.

Perhaps I haven't loved in a long time, so long that I've lost myself.

I believe in a little of a lot, love little a lot vs love a little of a lot. I
can't see how taste first can work for me. But at my happiest I loved Ivan, and
Sam, and Leo a lot. And that was all I needed. (referred to Ivan, and Sam, and Leo
to guide me.) But I no longer have something to guide me. I have myself perhaps
too much. More than other people. Because I liked my values at some point, and now
I've forgotten them. Something about taste, something about bringing people up.
[one word underlined and crossed out]

With the people around me now I only feel I have something to give. I have nothing
to take. Therefore my energy is drained.

Real relationships are bidirectional. I must feel they have something I can
connect with. That's the emptiness. [crossed out] Walden never loved.

I can love a lot a little. But it's exhausting and I have to focus. It's draining.
Moves from my own taste.

--- 3879 ---

Just like anyone else, I am looking for love to open me up. At some point recently
I shut down. I stopped playing and being. I turned completely inward. Perhaps
because I felt the outside world didn't have enough to offer; perhaps I need more
external challenges and experiences to show me otherwise. I am sure spring will
give me new pathways to love.

As for being active, this also perhaps relates to turning too inwards. I lost my
worldly attention. Stopped asking for help, for feedback, for connection.

This spring, we live outwardly, loudly. And use this for inner inspiration. Not
the other way.
